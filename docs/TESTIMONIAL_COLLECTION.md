# Client Testimonial Collection Guide

## Email Template to Request Testimonials

### Subject: Quick favor? 30-second testimonial for our portfolio

```
Hi [Client Name],

Hope you're doing well! 

I'm reaching out because we're updating our portfolio and website. Would you be willing to share a brief testimonial about your experience working with us on [Project Name]?

It would really help other potential clients understand what it's like to work with our team.

If you're open to it, here are a few quick questions (feel free to answer any or all):

1. What was the main challenge we helped you solve?
2. How was the communication and collaboration?
3. What results did you achieve?
4. Would you recommend us to others?

Even just 2-3 sentences would be incredibly helpful!

I can also draft something based on our project for your approval if that's easier.

Thanks so much for considering this!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
LamGame Studio
```

## Questions to Ask Clients

1. **Background**
   - What was your project about?
   - Why did you choose to work with us?

2. **Experience**
   - How was the communication?
   - How did we handle challenges or changes?
   - What did you like most about working with us?

3. **Results**
   - What results did you achieve?
   - Did we meet your expectations?
   - Would you work with us again?

4. **Recommendation**
   - Would you recommend us? Why?
   - What type of projects should others hire us for?

## Legal Considerations

1. **Always get permission** before using testimonials publicly
2. **Written consent** is best (email confirmation is fine)
3. **Anonymization options:**
   - "Marcus T., Indie Studio, Germany" (first name + initial)
   - "Project Manager, Game Studio, EU"
   - With permission: Full name and company

### Permission Request Template
```
Hi [Client Name],

Thank you so much for the testimonial! Before we use it on our website and marketing materials, I wanted to confirm:

[ ] I give permission to use my testimonial publicly
[ ] Use my full name: [Name]
[ ] Use my title: [Title]
[ ] Use my company name: [Company]
[ ] Use my country: [Country]

Or if you prefer:
[ ] Use first name + initial only (e.g., "Marcus T.")
[ ] Keep company name anonymous (e.g., "Indie Studio")

Please just reply with your preferences.

Thanks again!
[Your Name]
```

## Platform-Specific Review Requests

### For Clutch Reviews
- Clutch does phone interviews with clients
- They verify the relationship
- More credible but requires more client effort
- Ask clients who are very satisfied

### For Upwork Reviews
- Automatic after project completion
- Ask client to leave review and rating
- Respond to all reviews professionally

### For Google Reviews
- Create Google Business Profile first
- Send direct link to review page
- Good for local SEO (Vietnam)
